Notes

What counts as what
Which part of the photo
The street the car is on, both curbs if you can see them. Ignore cross streets in the distance.
Signs
Arrêt interdit = no stopping. Stationnement interdit = no parking. Livraison seulement / Débarcadère = loading. Payment sign or meter = paid. Réservé, permit zones (S3R, "excepté permis"), taxi, hotel, disabled = reserved. Time limits ("P 60 min") and anything else = other. Tick a type once even if there are several.
Parked at curb
Stopped against the curb, legal or not.
Double parked
Stopped in a traffic lane beside the curb lane or beside parked cars, not waiting in traffic.
In bike lane / in bus stop
Stopped on or across the bike lane, or at the curb in a bus stop. Count each vehicle in one position only.
Delivery van
Cargo van: Sprinter, Transit, ProMaster, courier vans. Passenger minivans are cars.
Box truck
Cube van or larger truck.
Loading
A person moving goods, a hand truck, or open cargo doors with goods.
Unusable
Too blurry, too dark, or the curb is not visible. Mark it and move on.
Keys
Enter = done and next. Left / right arrows = back / skip. Z = zoom (or click the photo).
